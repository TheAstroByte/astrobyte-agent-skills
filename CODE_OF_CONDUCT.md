# Code of conduct

Keep participation respectful and focused on the work. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

- Critique ideas and code; address people with respect.
- Welcome different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy and consent. Share only information you are authorized to disclose.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, personal attacks, spam, and repeated disruption.

The repository owner may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, or restrict participation. Apply the same standards to human and agent-assisted contributions; contributors remain responsible for what they submit.

For abusive content, use GitHub's [reporting controls](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam), including its support route. For a project-specific concern that needs private handling, ask the maintainer for a private contact route without posting names or incident details publicly. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
